The short version

Your visit belongs to you.

How It Runs has no accounts, cookies, advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking.

What stays on your device

Your current system and lever positions are written only into the page URL so you can copy a share link. We do not store them in a database or browser storage.

Network and offline use

After a visit, a service worker may cache the application’s public files on your device so it can work offline. You can remove this cache through your browser’s site-data controls.

Hosting records

Like most web hosts, our hosting provider may process short-lived technical logs such as an IP address, requested page, and browser type to deliver and protect the site. We do not use those logs to profile children or visitors.
