The short version

Your visit belongs to you.

There are no accounts, analytics, cookies, or saved profiles.

What stays on your device

Outside demo mode, the controls write settings only into the page URL. Demo mode writes sample data to one session-storage key.

Network and offline use

After the first visit, the browser caches public site files for offline use. Browser site-data controls can remove that cache.

Hosting records

Our host may process short-lived technical logs to deliver and protect the site. We do not use those logs to profile visitors.
