The short version

Your visit belongs to you.

How It Runs has no accounts, cookies, advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking.

What stays on your device

Your current system and controls are written only into the page URL. We do not store them in a database or browser storage.

Network and offline use

After a visit, a service worker may cache public app files so the current simulation can work offline. You can remove this cache through your browser’s site-data controls.

Hosting records

Our host may process short-lived technical logs to deliver and protect the site. We do not use those logs to profile visitors.
