Friendly ground rules

Learn, explore, and stay curious.

How It Runs is a free educational experience. It simplifies real systems to build intuition; it is not professional engineering or safety guidance.

Using the simulations

You may use and share the site at home or in a classroom. Do not rely on its numbers to operate, design, or make decisions about a real water plant, electrical grid, bakery, or other system.

Availability

We aim to keep the site useful and available, but it is provided “as is” without a promise that it will always be available or error-free.
